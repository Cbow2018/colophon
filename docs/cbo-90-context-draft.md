# CONTEXT.md additions — CBO-90 (draft)

Two terms settled in CBO-90's grilling (2026-09-24, Q7). Merge them under
`## Language` in the root `CONTEXT.md`, beside CBO-68's **Work**, **Edition** and
**Standard Edition** (`context-additions-cbo-68.md`). They depend on those three and
do not change them.

**Reading Format**:
The kind of thing an Edition is (physical, audio or ebook), as the source states it.
_Avoid_: edition format, format, medium

**Audio Edition**:
An Edition that the source states has an audio Reading Format. It is still an Edition
of its Work.
_Avoid_: audiobook edition, non-ebook edition
